## Name
Springforth

## Tagline
Community coordination infrastructure: declare possibilities, pool commitments, fund each other — with evidence.

## Description (public-goods framing)
Springforth is open-source public infrastructure for community coordination and funding. People declare "I want to…" possibilities with what they bring and need; others opt in; coordination activates at quorum; mutual appreciation builds a pairwise trust graph that improves matching and distributes surplus.

Funding mechanisms are public prior art implemented non-custodially: quadratic funding (Allo), dominant-assurance pledges (claimable balances — trustless refund if quorum fails), retro pools, and streaming support. Outcomes attach to attested evidence — EAS attestations, hash-chained ledger, OpenTimestamps Bitcoin anchoring, IPFS content-addressed evidence packs, and a multi-source oracle fusing satellite/field data.

Live at springforth.widespread.workers.dev. AGPL-3.0-only, ~4% custom code over verified upstream (PROVENANCE.md). Integrates Giveth, Karma GAP, Open Collective, Restor, Regen, Silvi, Bloom Network, OnePlanet.

## Why public goods
The infrastructure itself is the public good: no custody, no lock-in, every mechanism documented and every transaction unsigned-calldata the user signs. Demonstrated on Mida Creek (64,699 mangroves, Treegens-verified evidence).
