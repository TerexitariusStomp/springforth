# Competitive position — and how we surpass

The claim is **composition + verifiability**, not scale. Every identified
competitor owns one or two layers of the loop; none run the full
**declare → quorum → council → evidence → community review → monetary
valuation → retro reward** chain. And where they do ship numbers, ours
are re-derivable in-page — theirs are not.

## The matrix

| Competitor class | What they ship | What they lack (that we ship) |
|---|---|---|
| **Threshold pledges** — Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Crowdfunder | Threshold-bound pledges; ~41.57% all-project success rate (Kickstarter published stats) | Resource needs (not just money), councils, open evidence, trust graph, multi-capital valuation, community-benefit framing |
| **Quadratic / retro funding** — Gitcoin Grants, Allo, Giveth, clr.fund, Octant | QF allocation, retro rounds, badgeholder ballots | Coordination *before* funding (quorum on needs + people), outcome verification, four-capitals valuation, per-card forums |
| **MRV / field evidence** — Open Forest Protocol, GainForest, Silvi-adjacent tooling | Field evidence, remote sensing, verification pipelines | Community formation, quorum mechanics, funding rails, deliberation surfaces |
| **Deliberation** — Decidim, Polis, Snapshot | Proposals, voting, threaded deliberation | Action/commitment layer, attested outcomes, money movement, **mode-appropriate decisions** — we record consent/advice/duocracy/empowered calls with mandatory rationale; they only vote |
| **Mutual aid / timebanks** — Hylo, timebanking platforms, mutual-aid boards | Needs/offers matching, community feeds | Quorum binding, funding, audit ledger, standards-aligned valuation, councils |

Each row is real and live in Springforth: the audit trail + ledger,
the capital-tagged valuation, the quorum-seated council, the
council-gated forum, and the appreciation-review trust loop.

## The five measurable surpass-gates

Every claim below is checkable on the deployed worker — no marketing
assertions:

1. **`quorumRate` vs a published baseline.** `/impact#results` renders
   our live quorum rate next to Kickstarter's published ~41.57%
   all-project success rate. Same shape of commitment (threshold →
   bound), directly comparable.
2. **Verifier replay.** Every monetary figure on every card expands into
   an in-page derivation: inputs, formula, source, tier
   (ledger-derived / declared / cited-estimate). No competitor lets a
   third party re-derive a rendered number without leaving the page.
3. **`evidenceCoverage`.** Percentage of quorum/completed cards carrying
   at least one open-evidence reading or attestation — rendered live on
   `/impact#results`. For most competitors this number is ~0.
4. **$0 cited monetary valuation per card.** `valueCoordinated` derives
   ISO-14008-aligned per-capital valuations from ledger + declared +
   cited inputs at render time — versus consultant-MRV engagements priced
   in the tens of thousands per project.
5. **Council formation rate.** Percentage of quorum cards with a seated
   council — a metric category we own outright; nothing comparable
   exists on pledge or QF platforms.

## The crowd-surfing gates (added with the transcript layer)

Four more live-computable numbers in `/metrics`, each mapped to a
competitor-class weakness:

6. **`questionResolutionRate`** — share of engaged-curiosity questions
   answered on activated cards. Deliberation platforms *discuss*; our
   uncertainties are indexed and metabolized — answerable by anyone,
   resolved on the record.
7. **`decisionRationaleCoverage`** — decided decisions carrying a
   recorded rationale. 1.0 by construction (resolve rejects empty
   rationale) — which is the point: the "why" is the auditable trail no
   vote tally preserves.
8. **`referralConversion`** — referred actors who joined or showed
   interest. Trust-routed growth: referrals travel the mutual-recognition
   graph, which is our sybil resistance where others buy identity scores.
9. **`compostedCount`** — expired cards released back to the commons
   (ecocycle creative destruction). Kickstarter keeps a graveyard of
   dead projects clogging search; ours leave the default deck while
   staying on the ledger for audit.
10. **`testResolutionRate`** — share of proposed test cards resolved
    passed/failed. Lean-startup tooling tracks experiments in private
    dashboards; ours are public, question-linked records anyone can
    propose and the council must close with a recorded inference.
11. **`ecocycle.scarcityTrapped` / `ecocycle.rigidityTrapped`** — live
    counts of cards caught in the two failure valleys. No competitor
    can even *see* its dying or unreported projects as a system signal;
    ours surfaces them on `/impact` and per-card.
12. **`participatory.total` / `actorsEnacted`** — enacted competence,
    not endorsements: role-scoped "knows by doing" entries derived from
    the ledger on quorum+ cards. LinkedIn counts claims; we count what
    the audit trail proves people did.

## Honest caveats

Where competitors genuinely beat us, we say so:

- **Scale & brand** — Kickstarter/Gitcoin have orders of magnitude more
  users and recognition.
- **Mobile polish** — we are server-rendered-first; no native app.
- **Custody rails** — cards/wallets/fiat on-ramps are mature elsewhere;
  we deliberately stay non-custodial (unsigned tx builders, external
  signing) which trades convenience for auditability.

The surpass claims stay confined to **capability and verifiability** —
which is what a standards advisor or treasury reviewer actually
evaluates.
