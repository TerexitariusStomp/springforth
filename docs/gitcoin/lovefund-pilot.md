**tl;dr** — a concrete answer to @owocki's open call in the bread thread: *"interested in a commitment pooling (or other non financial) experiment on love.fund."* Proposing a pilot where Springforth — a live commitment-pooling + funding + evidence platform — runs one local pilot under the love.fund brand.

## What commitment pooling looks like in practice

Instead of "project posts a funding target and donors send money," participants declare *possibilities*: I want to do X, I bring A, I need B, this activates when N people commit. Commitments can be capital **or real-world resources** — the running instance has already matched boats, propagules, and transport against a coastal planting action. Coordination activates at quorum. Completion is attested against physical evidence, not self-report.

That's the Grassroots Economics commitment-pool lineage — and we already read Sarafu/community-pool activity as a live evidence source.

## Against the pilot checklist

**Where's the money coming from?** Honest answer: we don't bring a named funder — that's the ask. Two candidate sources worth discussing: (a) the Bread Cooperative endowment's yield, if this pilot is run under that umbrella, or (b) hackathon-ecosystem matchers — we're mid-flight in the Sustainable Finance Live 2026 hackathon (submissions Oct 27) with sponsors already orbiting. We're proposing the *mechanism and the venue*, not pretending the check is written.

**Where's the audience coming from?** Three live communities we can route: the 88-person SF Live hackathon cohort (active this week, money-motivated, submitting coordinated work), Grassroots-Economics-adjacent community-currency operators, and the Treegens/Silvi field-evidence network already publishing attested impact to the platform.

**One outcome we can check.** One possibility reaching quorum → resources delivered → completion attested on a public, hash-chained ledger anchored to Bitcoin (`/ledger/verify`, 2,163 events today — anyone can recompute it). Success is binary and externally checkable.

## What the pilot proves that the crowdfunding lane doesn't

- **Non-financial capital is real, not rhetorical.** The matched-resource ledger shows *things*, not just tokens.
- **"Match exists before it's promised"** is enforced in escrow logic (dominant-assurance pledges refund donors if quorum fails) — the Artizen liability structurally cannot form.
- **Many-to-many trust accrues.** Post-completion appreciation builds a `min(a→b,b→a)` mutual-recognition graph that improves the next round of matching — the platform gets smarter as it gets used.
- **Impact claims are checkable.** Hypercert-shaped claims backed by filmed field evidence and satellite oracles — the GG23 nature-stewards pattern, with stronger verification.

Live at `springforth.widespread.workers.dev`, AGPL-clean, under 4% custom code over public/MIT components, non-custodial (we build unsigned transactions for Celo/Allo/EAS/hypercert/Superfluid lanes; wallets sign). Being submitted to SF Live this week — so there's also a forcing function on polish.
