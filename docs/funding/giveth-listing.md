## Title
Springforth — coordination & funding infrastructure for communities

## Description
Communities declare possibilities — "I want to restore this creek, launch this market, fund this tool" — with what they bring, what they need, and a quorum. Springforth activates coordination at quorum, matches resources, and runs the funding: quadratic rounds, dominant-assurance pledges with trustless refunds, retroactive pools, and streaming support.

Everything verifiable: a hash-chained public ledger, EAS attestations, Bitcoin-anchored timestamps, IPFS evidence packs, and oracle readings from satellite + field sources. Live demonstrator: Mida Creek mangrove restoration (64,699 trees planted — evidence chain on the site).

Fully open source (AGPL-3.0), non-custodial — no funds ever held by the platform; transactions settle on public rails (Celo, Stellar).
