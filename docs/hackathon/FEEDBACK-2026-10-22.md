# Field feedback — 2026-10-22

Source: 18-minute voice note ("Springforth feedback.m4a"), reviewed after the
SF Live 2026 demo walkthrough. Distilled to the product decisions it implies.

## What landed in this pass

- **"How it works" moved to the top of the landing page** — right after the
  hero. First-time visitors need the concept before the information feed.
- **Attention strip re-triaged** — possibilities closest to quorum come first
  ("what's about to pop off"). The unmet-needs counter was dropped from the
  strip entirely: uncoordinated demand is noise to a first-time visitor and
  reads as a distraction. Unmet needs still live at `/collaborate#needs`.
- **Card-first feed on mobile** — `/possibilities` under 40rem renders a
  lightweight decision feed: status, %-to-quorum, place, timing. Each card
  carries **I'm interested / Not now** — "interested" opens the detail overlay
  (the existing `:target` mechanism, no JS), "not now" advances to the next
  card. Needs lists are hidden at this level — detail is interest-gated.
- **Interest feed** — `/possibilities?for=<actor>` (or the "only cards I'm in"
  filter chip when `?actor=` is set) shows only the possibilities an actor has
  joined: progress and quorum transitions land there. Search is hidden on
  mobile — not needed at first contact; it stays available on desktop and
  returns to mobile when information density warrants it.
- **Preserved** — declaring possibilities/needs/resources, participant
  coordination (Telegram bot + discourse links), post-completion
  appreciation/evaluation feeding the trust graph. Search field still present
  for desktop; `?actor=` personalization still sorts by trust edges.

## Direction noted, not yet built

- **Messaging/discourse opens at assembly** — don't offer a public channel
  before a possibility has enough committed weight to act ("you don't need a
  group chat for a thing that doesn't exist"). Current implementation: the
  Telegram bot is the coordination surface; gated per-possibility channels
  are future work.
- **Interest feed learns from yes/no** — passes already feed `viewerDeck`
  ordering; deeper personalization (feed ranking shaped by pass/join history
  plus appreciation graph) is the next iteration.
- **Desktop keeps the dense multi-surface view as a power-user layer** —
  the primary path stays the lightweight feed.
