# Field feedback — 2026-10-22

Source: 18-minute voice note ("Springforth feedback.m4a"), reviewed after the
SF Live 2026 demo walkthrough. Distilled to the product decisions it implies.

## What landed in this pass

- **"How it works" moved to the top of the landing page** — right after the
  hero. First-time visitors need the concept before the information feed.
- **Attention strip re-triaged** — possibilities closest to quorum come first
  ("what's about to pop off"). The unmet-needs counter was dropped from the
  strip entirely: uncoordinated demand is noise to a first-time visitor and
  reads as a distraction. Unmet needs still live at `/collaborate#needs`.
- **Card-first feed on mobile** — `/possibilities` under 40rem renders a
  lightweight decision feed: status, %-to-quorum, place, timing. Each card
  carries **I'm interested / Not now** — "interested" opens the detail overlay
  (the existing `:target` mechanism, no JS), "not now" advances to the next
  card. Needs lists are hidden at this level — detail is interest-gated.
- **Interest feed** — `/possibilities?for=<actor>` (or the "only cards I'm in"
  filter chip when `?actor=` is set) shows the possibilities an actor has
  joined **or marked interested in**: progress and quorum transitions land
  there. Search is hidden on mobile — not needed at first contact; it stays
  available on desktop and returns to mobile when information density
  warrants it.
- **Preserved** — declaring possibilities/needs/resources, participant
  coordination (Telegram bot + discourse links), post-completion
  appreciation/evaluation feeding the trust graph. Search field still present
  for desktop; `?actor=` personalization still sorts by trust edges.

## Second pass — the yes/no signals became real

- **"Not now" records a pass** — the feed's skip action POSTs
  `/possibilities/:id/pass` when a viewer is known (`?actor=`), then 303s to
  the next card via a sanitized `next` param. The pass feeds `viewerDeck`
  exclusion — the feed actually learns from "no". Anonymous viewers keep the
  plain anchor (no identity, nothing to attribute a pass to).
- **"I'm interested" records an Interest** — new `Interest` entity +
  `POST /possibilities/:id/interest`. The warm signal short of joining now
  persists, lands a `possibility.interested` ledger event, and redirects into
  the card overlay — interest-gating preserved, signal captured.
- **Feed actions on all viewports** — the interested/not-now pair shows on
  desktop too, not just the mobile feed; the dense grid remains as detail,
  the decision row is the primary path everywhere.
- **Since-engagement markers** — on the `?for=` feed each card compares its
  audit trail against the viewer's earliest engagement (join or interest):
  "N new since you engaged — reached quorum" covers joins, quorum, council
  seating, forum posts, and report filing.
- **"You're watching this"** — reason line for interested-not-joined
  viewers, plus a public count-only "N watching" chip on the card overlay
  (identity gradation preserved — count is public, names are not).

## Direction noted, not yet built

- **Messaging/discourse opens at assembly** — don't offer a public channel
  before a possibility has enough committed weight to act ("you don't need a
  group chat for a thing that doesn't exist"). Shipped shape: the
  quorum-gated per-card forum opens at assembly, council-gated writes; the
  Telegram bot remains the pre-assembly coordination surface.
